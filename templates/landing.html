The Industrial Challenge

Silent Danger Meets Next-Gen Vision

Hydrogen Sulfide (H₂S) causes olfactory fatigue within seconds at dangerous concentrations. Traditional electronic dosimeters require frequent recalibration and are costly, while analog chemical badges lack automated digital logging.

1. The Problem

H₂S is rapidly toxic, corrosive, and flammable. In refineries, wastewater utilities, and drilling rigs, workers face subtle shift increments that silently accumulate, increasing risks of chronic pulmonary and neurological harm.

2. Our Solution

H₂SENSE pairs wearable colorimetric indicator badges with optical Computer Vision. Workers perform 5-second check-in and check-out scans. The platform detects subtle chromatic shifts, calibrates for ambient temperature and humidity, and logs cumulative exposure.

3. Actionable Insights

Shift subtraction eliminates baseline noise. Plant safety officers gain a live bird's-eye view of high-risk personnel, department exposures, badge expiration statuses, and compliance audits through persistent MySQL records.

End-to-End Pipeline

How H₂SENSE Operates

An honest, scientifically sound architecture that separates digital demonstration from certified industrial sensors.

1. Sensor Color
Colorimetric chemical response (simulated digitally for prototype).
2. Optical Capture
Webcam or image upload with automated central ROI segmentation.
3. OpenCV Vision
Extracts dominant color, converts RGB to perceptual CIELAB (L*, a*, b*).
4. Calibration & Env
Inverse-distance interpolation & T/H environmental compensation.
5. Shift Subtraction
Exit reading minus Entry reading = exact shift exposure.
6. MySQL Persistence
WAMP database stores immutable records for occupational records.
Computer Vision & CIELAB Color Space

Cameras perceive light in non-linear RGB that varies across screen types and illuminations. H₂SENSE applies OpenCV to isolate a central 45% Region of Interest (ROI), eliminating specular reflections and outer shadows.

  • CIE L*a*b* Conversion: Separates perceptual lightness (L*) from chromatic coordinates (a* green-red, b* blue-yellow).
  • Color Distance ΔE: Calculates Euclidean color variance relative to known laboratory calibration points.
  • Glance-Proof Margin Filtering: Mitigates user hand jitter and peripheral lighting gradients.
CIE L*a*b* Distance Metric:
ΔE* = √[(ΔL*)^2 + (Δa*)^2 + (Δb*)^2]
Environmental Compensation Layer

Real-world colorimetric reactions accelerate with higher temperatures and elevated relative humidity. The H₂SENSE compensation service applies a mathematical correction baseline:

Prototype Correction Formula:
E_corrected = E_raw + 0.35 × (T - 25°C) + 0.15 × (H - 50%)
